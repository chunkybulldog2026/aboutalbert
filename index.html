01

Port of Long Beach

Protecting public trust during an 18-day maritime emergency

Crisis communicationsMessagingMedia relations

Challenge

After cargo containers fell from the containership Mississippi at Pier G, dramatic footage spread quickly while the Port needed to make clear that the incident was localized and the broader complex remained operational.

My role

Supported crisis communications by helping develop messaging, coordinate media interviews, monitor coverage and assist with communications throughout the Coast Guard-led Unified Command response. I later drafted the PRSA LA crisis communications award submission documenting the program.

Response

The communications strategy centered on verified facts, one coordinated voice and a steady update cadence. The first verified statement was issued less than three hours after the incident, with messaging reinforcing safety, transparency and continuity of Port operations.
